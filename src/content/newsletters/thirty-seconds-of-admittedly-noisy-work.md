This weekend I’m very mentally tired, for reasons that may become clear soon. (That I’m supposed to be voting on 14 California state ballot propositions, 10 San Francisco city propositions,[^prop] and a regional transit measure doesn’t help.) So this will be a low-effort post, sorry! And this post is... Recent Purchases I’ve Liked.

- [Peak Design City Crescent (6L)](https://www.peakdesign.com/products/city-crescent?Size=6L&Color=Black): Peak Design, producers of bougie, arguably-overpriced-arguably-not camera bags, has recently introduced a line of cheap(er), mainstream “city bags.” These bags, particularly the 6L City Crescent, left the typical travel-and-bag influencer types salivating, which left me slightly skeptical — is it _really_ that good, or are they just good at marketing? But, uh, it really is a very, very good bag. The 6L size is big enough to actually carry everything you need for a day out in the city — not least a full liter of water — while still being small enough not to get in the way. It’s got a magnetic pocket on the outside that’s perfect for sunglasses, and there’s a bag hook in a side pocket that lets you hang the bag from a table instead of stuffing it on the ground. It also has a tiny little AirTag pocket that’s pretty cute. It is, basically, my perfect day-to-day bag. The one thing that makes me hesitate is that I’m now seeing a $90 price — I’m pretty sure it was only $75 when I picked it up a few weeks ago? Also, they keep selling out. So maybe wait for a sale?
- [Belkin 10K BoostCharge Plus](https://www.belkin.com/p/10k-usb-c-power-bank-with-integrated-cables/BPB006btBLK.html): I recently discovered I owned no functional power banks, so I panic-bought this Belkin before heading on a trip involving a strenuous all-day hike. It’s a power bank! It’s literally just a solid power bank. But! It has a USB-C cable built-in, so you don’t have to carry anything else, which is pretty charming.
- [Hoto Air Pump Pocket](https://hototools.com/products/air-pump-pocket): I literally just bought this,[^sale] and I’ve only used it once, so consider this a conditional endorsement.[^friend] It’s a handheld, li-ion-battery-powered air compressor for bike tires — hook it up to the tire valve, select the right pressure, press start, and after about thirty seconds of (admittedly noisy) work, the tire is correctly inflated! I was using a foot pump before, which was a huge hassle — not least because my bike is hanging in a bike room and had to be unlocked and propped up every time I used it. This is much easier.
- [Muji Side Seamless Double Gauze Pajamas](https://www.muji.us/products/side-seamless-double-gauze-pajamas-fb0yd26a): So I had a set of Muji’s flannel pajamas, and while I liked them in _theory_, I always overheated while wearing them. But, luckily, on my last trip to Canada, a quick trip to Muji revealed the existence of their double-gauze pajamas, which are light and breathable and comfortable all night long.
- [Damita Thread Cutter Set](https://amazon.com/dp/B0G1MGBSZW): You may recall that I took a [sewing bootcamp](https://rwblickhan.org/newsletters/a-constant-sense-of-what-urban-dwellers-choose-to-see-and-unsee/) a couple months ago. One thing I learned that is that stitching is much easier to cut with spring-loaded thread snips. So I bought thread snips (in a full thread cutter set) so that I can remove all the stray stitching from my ~~low quality fast fashion~~ Uniqlo pants.
- [Thursday Boots Premier Low Top](https://thursdayboots.com/products/mens-premier-low-top-sneaker-coffee): I wanted a nice pair of brown sneakers to replace my last pair of sneakers (rapidly falling apart) and Thursday Boots has yet to disappoint. Now, I recently saw an alarming Reddit post that money sent to Thursday Boots is immediately turned into anti-Roe-vs-Wade PAC money, but after further research, my understanding is that the cofounder is basically an old-money Yale type who was in a social club related in some not-totally-direct way to Kavanaugh and friends. So, I think (?) it is not totally unethical to buy Thursday Boots, which is nice, because they do have a pretty nice quality for their price point.
- [Ikea Omtänksam shoehorn](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/p/omtaenksam-shoehorn-anthracite-70378070/): But you [knew that already](https://rwblickhan.org/newsletters/i-might-still-be-dehydrated/).
- [Xteink X4](https://www.xteink.com/): But you [knew that one too](https://rwblickhan.org/newsletters/distraction-free-eye-strain-free/).
